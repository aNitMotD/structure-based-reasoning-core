## Meta: Role–Thinking Separation  

- Principle:  
  Role (job role) and Thinking belong to different layers.  

- Structure:  
  Thinking Engine (invariant)  
        ↓  
  Role / Context (variable)  
        ↓  
  Output (observable)  

- Constraint:  
  Thinking cannot be directly inferred based on Role.  

- Common Failure:  
  Role–Thinking Collapse  
  → an interpretation error that collapses the distinction between job role and thinking,  
    and thereby identifies the job role with thinking.  

- Cause:  
  - invisibility of internal state (observability limit)  
  - minimal model inference (minimal inference)  
  - temporal alignment assumption (temporal alignment assumption)  

- Example (QA Case):  
  - Observation:  
    “The user has worked in QA since 2020.”  

  - Common Interpretation:  
    “The user’s way of thinking was formed around QA from 2020.”  

  - Structural Correction:  
    The thinking engine existed prior to the job role,  
    and the role of QA merely limited the output form of that thinking engine in a particular direction.  

- Implication:  
  Role does not define thinking,  
  and only limits the form of output.  

- Boundary:  
  Thinking is not directly observable,  
  and Role is only an observable output constraint condition.  

- Correlation Clause:  
  Observable job history may have a certain correlation with output patterns.  
  However, that correlation does not justify direct inference or assertion about internal thinking structure.  

- Non-Inference Clause:  
  Internal thinking structure cannot be determined or asserted  
  from observable output information.  

- Summary:  
  A job role does not create thinking.  
  It only limits how it will be revealed.  

## Meta: Method–Trait Separation  

- Principle:  
  Method (learning·performance method) and Trait (trait·capability state) belong to different layers.  

- Analytical Structure:  
  Method / Procedure (observable)  
        ≠  
  Trait / Capability / Knowledge State (internal)  

- Constraint:  
  Based on an observable Method,  
  an internal Trait, Capability, or Knowledge State cannot be directly determined.  

- Common Failure:  
  Method–Trait Collapse  
  → an interpretation error that collapses the distinction between a specific learning·performance method  
    and an internal understanding·knowledge·capability state,  
    and thereby treats the presence or absence of that Method  
    as identical to the presence or absence of that internal state.  

- Example (Learning Case):  
  - Observation:  
    “The user did not, over a long period, go through lectures, tutorial repetition, or direct implementation of the relevant technology.”  

  - Common Interpretation:  
    “Therefore, the user does not know or understand the relevant technology.”  

  - Structural Correction:  
    A learning method is not the internal knowledge·understanding state itself.  
    After grasping the core principle and securing the structural position of the technology,  
    referring to an external guide for the necessary use procedure is also  
    one Method of approaching the same target.  

- State Distinction:  
  The state of identifying the target and grasping the core principle,  
  the state of being able to operate it according to a purpose,  
  the proficiency state in which stable performance is repeatedly possible,  
  and the expert state in which deep problems and exceptions can be handled are not identical to one another.  

  Therefore, the fact that structural understanding exists  
  does not automatically extend to proficiency or deep problem-solving capability.  
  Conversely, the fact that a specific learning·performance path was not followed also  
  does not mean the absence of structural understanding.  

### Note: Learning–Experience–Know-how  

- Formula Recall and Derivation:  
  Memorizing the quadratic formula and calling it immediately,  
  and deriving the formula again from already-learned algebraic principles,  
  are different Methods.  

  The observation that the formula was not memorized  
  may indicate the absence of an immediate retrieval path,  
  but does not negate understanding of the principle or the possibility of reconstructing the result.  

- Derivation Depth:  
  The depth level of a derivation is not determined by the Method of ‘having derived it’ itself.  
  It varies according to the principles already provided at the starting point,  
  the structural distance to the point of arrival,  
  and whether the intermediate structures that were not provided were generated by oneself.  

- Knowledge–Experience Linkage:  
  Learned knowledge,  
  until it is connected with invocation conditions·application boundaries·failure conditions in actual situations,  
  may still remain in an inactive state.  

  Field experience is not merely time of exposure to the field,  
  but includes the process of linking events with existing knowledge  
  and modifying that linkage through exceptions and failures.  

  Learned Knowledge (inactive)  
        +  
  Field Events / Constraints  
        +  
  Person-internal Linkage / Revision  
        ↓  
  Person–Context-coupled Know-how  

- Checklist Case:  
  From the fact alone that the same checklist was performed for a long time,  
  it cannot be known whether situations outside the procedure can be judged  
  or whether the necessary procedure can be reconstructed.  

  - Procedure-bound Execution:  
    Within the given items and sequence,  
    speed·accuracy·local know-how are improved.  

  - Principle-linked Operation:  
    The purpose and constraints of each item are understood,  
    and when the situation changes, the procedure is selected·transformed·replaced·reconstructed.  

- Know-how Distinction:  
  Both states may have Know-how in a broad sense,  
  but their categories are different.  

  - Execution Know-how:  
    local execution knowledge that increases performance quality within a given procedure  

  - Judgment / Procedure-Construction Know-how:  
    person-internal operational knowledge that, according to purpose·principle·constraints,  
    selects an appropriate procedure or newly constructs one  

- Know-how Transfer Boundary:  
  Know-how itself cannot be directly transferred between persons.  
  What can be transferred is the material and conditions for reconstructing know-how,  
  such as explicit knowledge, principles, constraints, cases, procedures, and failure records.  
  The recipient links those materials with their own experience  
  and newly constructs their own know-how internally.  

- Relation to Method–Trait Separation:  
  The number of lectures, whether a formula was memorized, the number of projects,  
  years of career, and the number of checklist repetitions are all observable Methods / Proxies.  
  However, none of them  
  alone determines the knowledge–field linkage state or procedural reconstruction capability.  

- Implication:  
  The presence or absence of a specific Method  
  does not alone prove the presence or absence of a specific Trait or Knowledge State.  

  Repeated learning, direct implementation, and long-term usage experience  
  may be grounds for proficiency and experience,  
  but do not by themselves define the entire knowledge·understanding·capability state regarding the target.  

- Boundary:  
  Method can be observed,  
  but the internal knowledge·understanding·capability structure cannot be directly observed by Method alone.  

  Therefore,  
  “how it was learned” and  
  “what is known, understood, and can be performed”  
  must be described separately.  

- Correlation Clause:  
  A certain correlation may exist between a specific learning·performance method and a specific capability state.  
  However, that correlation does not justify  
  directly inferring or asserting Trait from Method.  

- Non-Inference Clause:  
  From an observable learning·performance method,  
  an internal knowledge·understanding·capability state  
  cannot be directly determined or asserted.  

- Summary:  
  A method is not a trait or capability state itself.  
  It is merely one of the paths through which that state is formed or revealed.  

## Structural Isomorphism  

- Terminological Boundary:  
  In this document, Collapse does not mean that either side itself breaks down.  
  It means that the distinction between two different layers is removed in interpretation,  
  so that an observable external variable is treated as identical to,  
  or sufficient to directly determine, an internal state.  

- Role–Thinking Collapse:  
  The distinction between observable Role / Context and internal Thinking is collapsed,  
  and internal Thinking is directly asserted from Role / Context.  

- Method–Trait Collapse:  
  The distinction between observable Method / Procedure  
  and internal Trait / Capability / Knowledge State is collapsed,  
  and the internal state is directly asserted from Method / Procedure.  

- Shared Structure:  
  Observable Proxy  
        ≠  
  Internal State  

  The two errors are structurally isomorphic  
  in that each collapses the distinction between an observable external variable  
  and an internal state that cannot be directly observed,  
  and then treats the former as identical to,  
  or sufficient to directly determine, the latter.
